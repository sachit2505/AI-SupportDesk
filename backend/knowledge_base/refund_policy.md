# Refund Policy

## Eligibility Guidelines
We strive for complete customer satisfaction. Our refund terms depend on subscription tier and billing frequency:

- **Annual Subscriptions**: Eligible for a 100% full refund within **14 calendar days** of the initial purchase or renewal date. Requests made after 14 days will be evaluated on a prorated basis for extenuating circumstances.
- **Monthly Subscriptions**: Monthly charges are non-refundable once the billing cycle begins. However, when canceled, access continues uninterrupted until the conclusion of the paid billing period.
- **One-Time Add-ons & Professional Services**: Non-refundable once work has commenced or credits have been utilized.

## Processing Times
- Approved refunds are credited back to the original payment method.
- Credit cards typically reflect the refunded credit within **5 to 7 business days**.
- Bank transfers or international wire payments may require **7 to 10 business days**.

## Requesting a Refund
To request a refund, submit a ticket under the **Billing** category with your Invoice Number or Transaction ID.
