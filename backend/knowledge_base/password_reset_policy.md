# Password Reset Policy

## Standard Password Reset Procedure
Users who have forgotten their password or need to reset their credentials can initiate a self-service reset:
1. Navigate to the login screen and click **"Forgot Password"**.
2. Enter the registered account email address.
3. A secure 6-digit verification code or one-time reset link will be sent to the email within 2 to 5 minutes.
4. Reset links are valid for **15 minutes** from issuance.

## Password Requirements
- Minimum 8 characters in length.
- Must include at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special character (!@#$%^&*).
- Cannot reuse any of the previous 3 passwords.

## Troubleshooting
If the reset email does not arrive:
- Verify your spam, junk, or promotions folder.
- Ensure the email address entered matches the primary account email.
- If corporate email filters block automated emails, whitelist `no-reply@supportdesk.internal`.
