# Subscription Cancellation Policy

## How to Cancel Your Subscription
Account owners can cancel an active subscription at any time without penalty:
1. Log in to your SupportDesk portal.
2. Navigate to **Settings > Billing & Plan**.
3. Under Current Plan, click **"Cancel Subscription"**.
4. Confirm cancellation on the prompt. You will receive an email confirmation immediately.

## Effect on Account Access & Data
- **Access Retention**: Upon cancellation, your account remains active on the current tier until the current billing cycle expires. No further charges will occur.
- **Downgrade to Free Tier**: After the billing period ends, the account will be transitioned to the Free Community plan.
- **Data Preservation**: Workspace data, tickets, and configurations are retained securely for **90 days** post-cancellation. You can reactivate anytime within this period to restore full functionality.
- **Data Deletion**: To request immediate permanent deletion of all data, contact our security and compliance team.
