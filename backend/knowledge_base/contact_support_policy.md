# Contact Support & Escalation Policy

## Support Channels & Availability
SupportDesk offers multiple channels for customer assistance:
- **Web Portal / Dashboard**: 24/7 ticket submission directly from your SupportDesk console.
- **Email Support**: Write to `support@supportdesk.internal` (automatically converts into a support ticket).
- **Live Chat**: Available for Pro and Enterprise subscribers Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM EST.

## Response Time SLA (Service Level Agreement)
Our response targets are based on ticket priority:
- **Urgent Priority**: Critical production outage or complete account lockout affecting business operations. Initial response within **1 hour**.
- **High Priority**: Major feature impairment, duplicate payment, or billing issue. Initial response within **4 hours**.
- **Medium Priority**: Standard questions, non-critical bugs, configuration queries. Initial response within **12 hours**.
- **Low Priority**: General product feedback, documentation inquiries. Initial response within **24 hours**.

## Escalation Path
If your issue is time-sensitive or unaddressed within the SLA window, reply to the ticket with the keyword `[ESCALATE]` or request senior agent review.
