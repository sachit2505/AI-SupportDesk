# Duplicate Payment Policy

## Identifying Duplicate Charges
If you notice two identical charges on your credit card statement or bank account for the same billing period:
1. Check whether one of the charges is an **authorization hold** (often marked "Pending"). Many banking apps temporarily show holds that drop off within 48 to 72 hours.
2. Verify if multiple workspaces or team members hold separate active subscriptions under different emails.

## Immediate Refund Guarantee for Accidental Duplicates
- If a genuine duplicate billing event occurred due to network retry, gateway timeout, or system glitch, SupportDesk will immediately issue a **full 100% refund** for the redundant charge.
- Processing is expedited within **24 hours** of ticket submission.
- The refund will automatically credit the payment method originally charged.

## Information Needed from Customer
When submitting a ticket for duplicate payment:
- Last 4 digits of the card charged.
- Exact dates and amounts shown on the statement.
- Transaction or invoice IDs from both receipts.
